# ALVIRA Dossier — Positioning and Product Role

## Decision

ALVIRA Dossier is **not a separate public product offering**. It is the private ownership/portability layer of ALVIRA Context Intelligence.

Public architecture:

- **ALVIRA Context** — what ALVIRA knows and maintains.
- **ALVIRA Reflect** — where the user examines, challenges, and evolves that understanding.
- **Context History** — how the understanding changed over time.
- **ALVIRA Dossier** — the private, encrypted representation the user controls.
- **Reuse / Bridge** — where the user chooses to put maintained Context to work.

## Public promise

> Your Context should belong to you.

> ALVIRA can maintain your Context without owning your Context.

The Dossier should answer the trust question that follows naturally from ALVIRA's value proposition: if ALVIRA learns increasingly useful context about a person or their work, can the user keep, inspect, restore, and selectively reuse that understanding without being locked to one conversation or one interface?

## What the Dossier is

A durable, portable representation of deliberately built and validated ALVIRA Context. The target artifact can include, as appropriate:

- confirmed Context;
- corrections and user-approved revisions;
- meaningful Context History;
- Reflect-derived understanding that the user has accepted;
- provenance/source references where useful;
- uncertainty/change metadata where appropriate;
- explicit sharing or authorization metadata.

## What it is not

- Not a transcript of everything a user has ever said.
- Not a hidden behavioral profile.
- Not permission for an AI or agent to act for the user.
- Not a reason to charge for baseline security.

## Security and pricing principle

Security/privacy of stored user information is not a premium feature. Paid Dossier differentiation, if any, should concern advanced portability, recovery, history, selective disclosure, interoperability, restore/import, and other incremental capabilities — not whether the user's information is protected.

Current intended commercial fit:

- **Free** — basic Context export/backup within Free-plan limits.
- **Pro** — full encrypted Dossier capability as it ships, including richer portability and restore/import.
- **Lifetime** — core Dossier capabilities included within the Lifetime ALVIRA scope.
- **Founding Beta** — full customer-facing Dossier capabilities as they become available during the beta.

Do not create a separate Dossier pricing card unless future evidence shows a genuinely distinct standalone/local/private product with independent value and cost.

## Shipping honesty

As of this positioning update, the public product already supports Context export/reuse patterns, but the complete encrypted Dossier, restore/import, and selective-disclosure system is **forthcoming**. Public copy must mark these advanced Dossier capabilities as being built/shipped in stages rather than implying they are already complete.

## MeOS origin

Preserve this origin story:

> MeOS began with the question of whether a person could hold a durable, private representation of themselves that intelligence could work from. The encrypted dossier was the original object. ALVIRA became the Context Intelligence system required to keep that object accurate, alive, inspectable, and useful over time.

Short internal shorthand:

**MeOS began as the dossier. ALVIRA became the system that keeps the dossier alive.**

This history belongs in the Build Journal/product archive even though MeOS is no longer a separate public product.
