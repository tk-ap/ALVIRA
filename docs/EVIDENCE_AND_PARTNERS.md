# ALVIRA Evidence & Company Narrative

## Current objective
ALVIRA is not treating acquisition as one funnel. The current job is to produce evidence that Context Intelligence creates persistent value.

## Core thesis
AI capability is increasing quickly, but useful context remains fragmented across products, sessions, and workflows. People and organizations repeatedly explain the same goals, history, constraints, preferences, projects, and operating context.

ALVIRA is building **Context Intelligence**: a user-controlled layer that learns, maintains, reviews, and selectively supplies the context AI needs to work effectively.

The strategic position is not that one AI should know everything about a person. People should be able to build useful Context, correct it, decide what belongs, and choose where it goes.

## Product loop
Interview → Context → Review/correct → Select → Bridge → AI tool → Measure → Context evolves.

## Two evidence tracks

### 1. Individuals — Context Lift Founding Beta
Question: **Does persistent user-controlled Context make AI more useful?**

Use the existing Founding Beta and Context Lift protocol. Measure activation, effectiveness, trust, portability, retention, willingness to pay, re-explanation, correction, and voluntary reuse.

The 20-person beta remains active. It is an evidence program, not the only acquisition strategy.

### 2. Organizations — Design Partner / POC
Question: **Does persistent Context improve a recurring AI workflow enough that an organization wants to keep using, integrate, or pay for it?**

A design partner should:
1. choose a real recurring workflow where useful context is repeatedly supplied to AI;
2. establish a baseline;
3. create/review the relevant ALVIRA Context;
4. selectively introduce that Context into the workflow;
5. compare outcomes;
6. document repetition saved, corrections, quality changes, trust issues, portability, integration requirements, and economic value.

A single strong organizational POC may provide deeper evidence than many lightweight signups because it can expose workflow frequency, multi-tool portability, trust requirements, economic value, and willingness to pay together.

## Acquisition surfaces

### ALVIRA
ALVIRA owns the product/business case. The dedicated /partners surface is for business owners, AI/product teams, potential design partners, and investors who need to understand the infrastructure thesis and proof plan quickly.

It should remain evidence-first: problem, Context Intelligence thesis, working product loop, what exists today, proof framework, individual and organizational evidence tracks, and a clear pilot CTA.

Do not turn the consumer homepage into an investor deck.

### ASHWOOD
ASHWOOD remains the founder layer: why TK is building this, the context-ownership thesis, relevant Build Journal evidence and decisions, and links into ALVIRA's company/design-partner material.

The pitch deck should be derived from the same canonical narrative rather than becoming a second, drifting story.

## Current claims boundary
Safe to demonstrate as current product capability:
- guided interview;
- Context creation;
- user review/correction;
- selective context use;
- Bridge/context transfer where implemented;
- the Context Lift measurement framework.

Do not present unmeasured customer outcomes, market adoption, ROI, or organizational value as established facts. Those are what the beta and design-partner tracks are intended to test.

## Near-term evidence hierarchy
1. A real organizational POC with measurable before/after evidence.
2. Repeated voluntary Context reuse by individual beta testers.
3. Context Lift results across structured beta experiments.
4. Qualitative feedback and signup interest.
5. Raw traffic or social engagement.

Traffic and Threads responses are acquisition signals, not product proof.
