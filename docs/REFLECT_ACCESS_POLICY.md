# Reflect access policy

Reflect is part of the ALVIRA Context lifecycle, not a second product slot.

- Free users receive the complete Reflect domain model for their one saved Context.
- Reflect records must not consume an additional Free saved-Context slot.
- Founding Beta users receive unrestricted customer-facing ALVIRA access and must bypass Free capacity/interview limits consistently.
- Founder/admin test mode is separate from the persisted `founding_beta` customer entitlement.
- Monetization boundaries should be capacity, sustained usage, and advanced operational features rather than reduced quality of understanding.
